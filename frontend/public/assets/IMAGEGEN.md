# Generated Attraction Artwork

These text-based SVG assets were created for the Borneo map attraction cards so the app can render project-owned imagery without relying on external stock-photo URLs.

## Assets

- `danum-valley.svg` — stylized lowland rainforest canopy scene with an orangutan focal point.
- `kinabatangan-river.svg` — stylized river corridor scene with warm water reflections, canopy foliage, a proboscis monkey, and hornbill silhouettes.
- `gunung-mulu.svg` — stylized limestone pinnacle and cave-mouth scene inspired by Gunung Mulu karst landscapes.

## Imagegen prompt

Use case: illustration-story
Asset type: attraction-card imagery for a Borneo travel web app
Primary request: create cohesive, stylized text-based SVG illustrations for Danum Valley, Kinabatangan River, and Gunung Mulu National Park.
Scene/backdrop: Borneo rainforest locations with dense canopy, tropical mist, river/cave/karst cues matching each attraction.
Subject: recognizable destination mood rather than photoreal documentation; include subtle wildlife where relevant.
Style/medium: polished editorial digital illustration, rich jungle greens, warm sunlight, cinematic depth.
Composition/framing: landscape card art with a clear central focal area and enough edge detail to crop gracefully.
Constraints: no logos, no watermark, no readable in-image text.
